# Slice Rush

A silly pizza-parlor race for phones, tablets, and computers. Run between the tables, grab slices before the regulars do, and get stuffed first.

Made by **Alex, Emma, Claude, and Niklas**.

## How to play

Slices pop up on plates around the pizzeria. Each slice you eat makes you fatter and fills your bar:

- Cheese: +1
- Pepperoni: +2
- Golden: +5

The first eater to reach 20 wins. Gino, Rosa, and Sal are racing you, and they get fatter too.

### Controls

- **Tap or drag** to run. Tap a table to dash to its slice.
- **Tap a regular** to bop them; they stumble for half a second.
- **Arrow keys or WASD** also work on a computer.

### Things that happen

- **Speed coin:** run faster for a few seconds.
- **Jump coin:** hop over tables (and gum) for a few seconds.
- **Chewing gum:** step in it and your shoes get sticky.
- **Wind storm:** after a 3-2-1 countdown, wind pushes everyone one way for 7 seconds.
- **Cheese storm:** after a 3-2-1 countdown, the floor turns gooey and everyone slows down for 3 seconds.
- **The cat:** about twice a game, a big striped cat leaps onto a table, eats a slice, and knocks everyone nearby dizzy. Watch for the red landing ring.
- **The crown:** whoever is in the lead glows and wears a crown.
- **Getting fat:** the fatter you are, the harder the others bounce off you. Near the end you are too wide for the narrow gaps between tables.

Pick Easy, Normal, or Hard on the start screen. The game remembers your fastest win for each level on that device.

## Running it

The whole game is one file, `slice-rush.html`, with no dependencies. Open it in any modern browser.

To put it on a website, upload the file (rename it `index.html` if you want it to load at a folder's address). Any static host works, for example a personal website, GitHub Pages, or Netlify.

On a phone or tablet, open the game and choose **Add to Home Screen** to launch it full screen like an app.
